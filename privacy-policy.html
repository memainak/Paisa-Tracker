Paisa Tracker — Privacy Policy Effective date: 22 August 2026 · Notice version: 3.1 Paisa Tracker ("the App") is operated by Ad-Venture(ad-venture.in) ("we", the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact: support@ad-venture.in. 1. Summary Paisa Tracker is an on-device expense tracker. Every expense, income entry, budget, loan, split, and receipt photo you create is stored on your device, and we cannot see your entries. Two optional features change that, and only if you switch them on: Google Drive backup. If you turn it on, a copy of your data is written to a private folder inside your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. It goes to your Google account, not to us; we never receive it and cannot read it. Competitions. If you sign in and join a competition, the other members of that group see your display name and the percentage of your income you saved that month. They never see your income, your spending totals, your individual entries, your categories, or anything else. Only the percentage is stored, in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which acts as our data processor — we run no server of our own for this. The database is configured so that a record carrying an amount is rejected outright, so amounts cannot reach it even by mistake. Groups are private and joinable only by an invite code you were given; they cannot be searched or browsed. Leaving a group deletes the figures you reported to it. If you use neither, nothing about your money leaves your device. Signing in is optional; the app is fully usable without an account. Data leaves your device only in these cases, each tied to a specific feature: Google AdMob — serves the ads that keep the App free. Google speech services — only when you tap the microphone to add an entry by voice. Google's geocoding service — only if you turn on automatic currency detection and grant location, to turn a location into a country. open.er-api.com — only when the in-app currency converter fetches exchange rates. No information about you is sent with the request. Firebase Cloud Messaging — so we can send occasional app news and tips. This registers an anonymous device token with Google. It is not linked to you and carries none of your entries. Firebase Analytics — records which screens and features are used, and basic device information, so we can see what to improve. It records that you opened a screen, never the amounts, categories, or notes on it. Firebase Crashlytics — sends a stack trace and device state when the App crashes, so we can fix it. Bank and UPI alerts stay on your device. If you switch on "Log from notifications", the App reads transaction alerts to find the amount — but that text is processed entirely on your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and never becomes an entry without your tap. 2. Reading bank and UPI alerts (optional) Paisa Tracker can offer to log a transaction when your bank or UPI app shows an alert, so you don't have to retype it. This feature is off by default and has deliberate limits: It does nothing until you switch it on in Settings → Read bank alerts and separately grant notification access in your Android system settings. Either one alone is not enough. Alert text is scanned on your device to find an amount. It is not sent to us, and we have no ability to receive it — the App contains no code that transmits it anywhere. Nothing is written to your records automatically. A recognised alert becomes a prompt that opens the normal entry sheet pre-filled, which you confirm or dismiss. You can revoke it at any time by turning off the setting, or by removing notification access in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications. The App does not hold the READ_SMS permission and never reads your message inbox. 4. Ads and consent The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Before ads load, we show Google's consent form (UMP) where you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change this choice at any time. If you decline personalisation, AdMob serves non-personalised ads, which still use the advertising ID for frequency capping and fraud prevention. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses advertising data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. 5. Your data and your rights (DPDP Act 2023) Because your entries live only on your device, you are in direct control of them: Access & correction: view and edit any entry directly in the App. Erasure: delete individual entries in the App, use Settings → Reset data to clear a period or everything, or remove all data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Paisa Tracker → Storage → Clear data. This permanently deletes everything, as we hold no server copy. Withdrawing consent: every optional feature can be switched off independently — notification reading, automatic currency detection, app news notifications, and usage/crash reporting in Settings, location and microphone in your Android permission settings, and ad personalisation through the in-app consent form. Switching off Settings → Usage data and crash reports stops analytics and crash collection on your device from that moment. Advertising data: managed through your device ad settings and the in-app consent choice above. Grievance: Grievance Officer — Mainak Sinha, mainak@ad-venture.in. Complaints are acknowledged immediately and answered within 15 days. If unsatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India. 6. Retention Your entries are retained on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We hold no server-side copy to retain or purge. Transaction alert text is not retained at all — it is examined for an amount and discarded; only an entry you confirm is saved. Exchange rates are cached on your device and refreshed periodically. Advertising data is retained by Google per its own policies, as are analytics events and crash reports (Firebase default retention; crash reports are kept up to 90 days). 7. Third parties Google AdMob — advertising; receives the advertising ID and ad-interaction data. Google privacy policy. Google speech services — used only when you tap the microphone to add an entry by voice. Your spoken audio is sent to Google to be converted to text, which is returned to the App and used to pre-fill the entry. If you never use voice input, no audio is collected or sent. Google privacy policy. Google geocoding service — used only if you have granted location permission. Android resolves your coordinates through a Google-provided service, either into a country (for currency detection, where the App keeps only the resulting currency choice) or into a short place name such as “Salt Lake, Kolkata” (for geo-tagging, where the App saves that name and the coordinates on your device with the entry). Geo-tagging can be switched off at any time in Settings → Currency; switching it off stops new entries being tagged and leaves existing tags in place, which you can remove by deleting those entries. If you leave location off, your SIM and region settings are used instead and no coordinates are involved. Google privacy policy. Firebase Analytics (Google) — records app usage events and device information against a Google-generated app-instance ID. We use it to see which features are worth investing in and which are being missed. It never receives your entries. Firebase privacy. Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — receives a stack trace and device state when the App crashes. Reports are technical diagnostics, not content: they describe what the code was doing, not what you had recorded. Firebase privacy. Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers the App's news-and-tips notifications. Google issues an anonymous registration token to your device so a message can reach it. We send messages to all subscribed devices at once and receive no list of who they are; we cannot target you individually, and no entry data is ever included. Switch it off in Settings → App news and tips and the token is unsubscribed. Firebase privacy. open.er-api.com — supplies exchange rates for the in-app currency converter. The App requests a fixed, public rate table; it sends no account, device, or entry information, and not even the currency you selected. As with any internet request, the operator of that service can see your IP address. Terms and privacy. The currency converter is a reference calculator only. It never alters your saved entries — amounts you have recorded are always kept exactly as you entered them. 8. Security Your data stays within the App's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app-sandbox. We recommend you keep a device screen lock enabled. As your financial entries are not transmitted to us, there is no server-side store of them to breach. 9. Children Paisa Tracker is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's data. 10. Changes We will note material changes in-app and update the notice version above.
